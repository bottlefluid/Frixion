My Account

Activity Status

Frixion shows your friends what you're doing, all on its own. It appears on your profile, in the Friends list and on the phone Home screen, and it goes away when you leave Frixion.

Share my activity
Turn this off and nothing about what you're doing is shown to anyone.
Show games and Spotify
The Frixion app notices the game you are playing and the Spotify song you are listening to on this computer. Turn this off to stop sharing them.
Games on your computer and your Spotify song are shared automatically when you use the Frixion app for computers.
What gets shared
  • Playing a game from the Games hub
  • Watching or Listening to a video or audio file you're playing in a chat
  • Listening to the Shop song you have equipped
  • In the Frixion computer app: the game you're playing and your Spotify song

Keyboard

Autocorrect
Fixes common typos as you type, on desktop and the on-screen keyboard on mobile.

Checks common English words only — it won't touch usernames, mentions, or custom emoji shortcodes.

Custom Keyboard
Use Frixion's own on-screen keyboard on mobile instead of your device's keyboard.
Quick Buttons
Show the Quick Text / Copy / Paste row above the custom keyboard.
Overlay shortcut
Opens your friends, DMs and servers on top of anything, including Quests games. Drag a window's top bar to move it and drag its edges to resize it. Click the shortcut, then press the new keys. Default is Shift + ` (the ~ key).

All keyboard settings are synced to your account.

Tapping one of these on the keyboard's Quick Text button sends it immediately — no need to type or press send.
